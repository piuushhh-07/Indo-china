About Indo China

Indo China – Family Restaurant is a Chinese restaurant on Shivaji Road in the Kansai Section of Ambernath, Maharashtra. We serve a full Indo-Chinese menu — soups, starters, momos, tandoor, sizzlers, rice and noodle dishes, Thai-style plates, and a separate Indian main-course and biryani menu — with vegetarian options clearly marked throughout.

We're rated 4.1 out of 5 across 690 Google reviews, and a meal for two typically runs ₹400–₹600.
